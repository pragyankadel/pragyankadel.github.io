Every data scientist eventually needs a place to think out loud. This is mine.

I built this site as a space to document my professional journey — not just as a portfolio, but as a living record of how I think, what I'm learning, and where I'm headed. If you're a recruiter, a collaborator, or just someone who stumbled here: welcome.

## What I'll Be Writing About

**Data science in practice.** The gap between theory and real-world data work is wide. I'll be sharing the experiments I run, the pipelines I build, the models that work and — more honestly — the ones that don't.

**New technologies.** The field moves fast. Whenever I pick up something new — a framework, a paper, a tool worth knowing — I'll write it up here while it's fresh.

**Research.** I'm interested in [your research area — edit this]. I'll use this blog to work through ideas, summarise papers I find compelling, and document any ongoing research projects.

**The job hunt.** I'm currently looking for my next role in data science and ML. I'll be open about that process — what I'm applying for, what I'm learning from interviews, and what the market looks like from where I'm standing.

## Why Blog at All?

Writing forces clarity. You can convince yourself you understand something until you try to explain it. This blog is as much for me as it is for anyone reading it — a way to consolidate what I know, track how my thinking evolves, and stay accountable to keeping sharp.

I'll aim to post regularly. Let's see how that goes.

— Pragyan
